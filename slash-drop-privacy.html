Privacy Policy - Slash Drop

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Slash Drop, developed and published by Corlix Gaming.

Information We Collect

Slash Drop does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

The game may store local gameplay information on your device, including settings, preferences, high scores, game progress, sound settings, music settings, and vibration settings.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used only to provide game functionality and remember your preferences.

Local Storage

Slash Drop uses Unity PlayerPrefs and similar local storage systems to save certain game settings and progress.

This information normally remains on your device unless the game is deleted, its data is cleared, or the operating system removes the data.

Third-Party Services

Slash Drop is developed using the Unity game engine. Unity and platform services such as Google Play may process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies.

You can learn more about Unity's privacy practices here: Unity Privacy Policy

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Slash Drop is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. Corlix Gaming does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

External Links

Slash Drop may contain links that open external services such as Google Play, privacy policy pages, terms of service pages, or email applications.

Corlix Gaming is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites and services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the game, its features, or applicable services.

Any updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

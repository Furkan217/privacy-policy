Privacy Policy - Slash Drop

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Slash Drop, developed and published by Corlix Gaming.

Information We Collect

Slash Drop does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

The game may store local gameplay information on your device, including settings, preferences, high scores, game progress, sound settings, music settings, and vibration settings.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide game functionality and remember your preferences.

Local Storage

Slash Drop uses Unity PlayerPrefs and similar local storage systems to save certain game settings and progress.

This information normally remains on your device unless the game is deleted, its data is cleared, or the operating system removes the data.

Advertising

Slash Drop uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process certain information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app interaction data, and advertising interaction data in accordance with Google's privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

Google may use this information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to the user's consent choices and applicable laws.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

Slash Drop may use third-party services that can process technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

These services may include:

  • Google AdMob - advertising
  • Google Play - app distribution and platform services
  • Unity - game engine and related technical services

Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by law, Slash Drop may request your consent before showing personalized advertisements or allowing certain advertising technologies to process data.

You may be able to manage certain advertising and privacy choices through the consent options shown in the game, your device settings, or your Google account settings.

Children's Privacy

Slash Drop is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. Corlix Gaming does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the game.

Third-party services used by the game may process limited technical data according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

External Links

Slash Drop may contain links to external services such as Google Play, privacy policy pages, terms of service pages, or email applications.

Corlix Gaming is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites and services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Slash Drop, its features, advertising services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.
